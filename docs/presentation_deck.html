Transduction Physics & Capacitive Coupling

Parallel-plate and fringing capacitance model: C = ε₀εᵣA/d + C_fringe

  • Skin/Tissue: ε ≈ 50–80 (high water/electrolyte content)
  • Medical Tube (PVC/silicone): ε ≈ 2.8
  • Air Gap: ε ≈ 1.0
  • Extubation / Peeling: Tissue replaced by air gap → Deep Negative Delta (ΔC << 0)
  • Touch / Press: External mass coupling → High Positive Delta (ΔC >> 0)

Architecture & Algorithm Selection

  • Classifier: HistGradientBoosting (150 trees, max depth 8, class_weight='balanced')
  • Features: 34 features = 25 spatial pad deltas + 9 statistics (spatial distribution preserves 2D detachment geometry)
  • Annunciator: 7-of-6 majority vote with 9-frame hold window
  • Accuracy: 97.53% leave-one-file-out cross validation across 81 bench files

Horizontal Pull Dynamics & Sensor Polarity

Horizontal tension produces compression / press signals rather than lift. The 34-feature gradient boosting model captures this multi-axis transition without hardcoded handcrafting.

Clinical Alarm Annunciation

3-Tier escalation architecture compliant with medical telemetry ergonomics:

  • Tier 3 (High - Red): 2.0 Hz flashing, Level 3 full detachment (5-pulse buzzer)
  • Tier 2 (Medium - Yellow): 0.6 Hz flashing, Level 2 peel/dislodgement (3-pulse beep)
  • Tier 1 (Information - Cyan): Steady illumination, Level 1 touch (gentle 2-note chime)
